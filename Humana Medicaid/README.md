## Findings
- Total Humana members in Florida: 813,254
- Total Medicaid members in Florida: 4,524,170
- Overall Market Share: 17.98%
- 49.62% of the medicaid members in Santa Rosa, FL have Humana
- Broward, FL has the largest number of Humana medicaid members(98,283), market share is 25.87%
- Miami-Dade, FL has 92,243 Humana medicaid members, the market share is 12.85%

## Recommendations
- Target Miami-Dade, FL for increased Humana participation
