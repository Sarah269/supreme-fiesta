## Findings
- Consumer & Retail, Auto & transportation, and Data management industries had the highest valuations.
- The number of unicorn companies skyrocketed from 2020 to 2021.
- Fintech and Internet & Software industries had 2 to 3 times more unicorns from 2019 to 2021 than the other industries.
- Fintech, E-commmerce, and Internet Software industries consistently had the most unicorns each year from 2019 to 2021.
