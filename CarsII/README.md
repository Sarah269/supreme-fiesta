# Cars II

## Project
Designed a dashboard to analyze dealership performance.


[Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/s.pfeiffer2269/viz/CarsII/Dashboard1)


## Data Source
- SAS Studio Cars dataset.  Modified the SAS cars dataset using Google Sheets.

## Tools
- SAS Studio, Google Sheets, Tableau

## Findings
- The dealership earned $1.5 million dollars in profit. The profit margin was 8.49%.
- Porsche and Lexus had the highest profit margins, 12.70% and 12.31% respectively.
- Ford, Mercedes-Benz, and Toyota had the highest number of cars sold.
- 39% of cars sold were made in Asia.
- 62% of cars sold were Sedans.
- Merceded-Benz C240 4dr was the best selling car by make/model.  The dealership sold 6 Merceded-Benz C240 4dr.
